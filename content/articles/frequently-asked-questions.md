{{< details summary="Why do I get the munchies?" >}}
Cannabinoids stimulate the release of a hunger molecule called ["ghrelin"](ghrelin).
Also food tastes better!
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Edibles vs. Smoking" >}}
When you inhale cannabis, you are inhaling Delta-9 THC, and goes from lungs to blood stream, so the effects are very quick. 

When consuming edibles, there's a bit more of an involved process, from stomach, to intestines, to liver, 
and it's in the liver where a hydroxy group is attached forming a new molecule to form: [11-Hydroxy-THC](11-hydroxy-thc), which will provide a slightly different feeling, and takes longer to hit.
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Resin vs. Live Resin vs. Rosin" >}}
Resin more general term for oils and extracts. 

Live Resin is cannabis that is flash frozen after harvest, and is not dried. So you have a more robust flavor profile. 

Rosin is pressed from the flower itself with just heat and pressure. No solvents or extraction. 
{{< /details >}}


## Science

{{< details summary="Why do hops for beer look like cannabis?" >}}
They are close relatives!
Both are in the family [Cannabinaceae](cannabinaceae).
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="THC vs. CBD" >}}
The feeling you get from these two [cannabinoids](cannabinoids) may differ, 
but they have a very similar molecular structure! They weigh the same and have the same number of Carbons, Hydrogens, and Oxygens.

The shape is different though, and that makes all the difference. 

Generally, [CBD](cbd) will not give you [psychedelic](psychedelic) effects, 
however, when combined with [THC](thc), many note that effects are stronger.
It's the [endo-cannabinoid system](endo-cannabinoid) after all, man! 
{{< /details >}}




{{< details summary="What are Trichomes" >}}
Trichomes appear only on female cannabis plants. 
This is where all of the cannabinoids and terpenes are synthesized.
They are found in the little head of the mushroom looking structure.
{{< /details >}}



{{< details summary="Why do you get 'high' from THC?" >}}
Anandamide molecule, from the sanskrit word for "joy"/"bliss".

This molecule is released in the brain and makes us feel happy,
but also maybe anxious and paranoid. 
{{< /details >}}

{{< details summary="Purple Weed" >}}
Purple weed is from molecules called ["anthocyanins"](anthocyanins).
Plants often produce anthocyanins when they are in cold environments. 
They can also help protect against UV.
