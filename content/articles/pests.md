Pests are an issue that plague all growers. 
Like most things, an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure. 
Once an infestation gets out of control, it can often be hard to recover. 

## Common Types of Pests


### Aphids
Aphids love to hang out on stems and eat delicious leaves.
They are tiny, soft-bodied insects that like to hang out in large groups. 
They generally amass on stems, or under leaves. 

In their adult stages, they are able to fly.

## Prevention and Solutions

### Neem Oil

Neem oil is a natural remedy that that can help prevent insects, but also certain fungus and mold. 
Be aware however that it has a bit of a foul scent.

It is created from neem tree seeds.

### Other Insects

Some insects are predators that can actually help control the pests that try to make a home on your plant. 

#### Lady Bugs
Lady bugs are a fantastic predator of invasive insects. 

Color: Varies (red, orange, yellow, pink).  
Markings: Can have many spots, few spots, or none; spots are symmetrical.  
Antennae: Short and clubbed (thickened at the end).  
Shape: Dome-shaped or hemispherical.  
Role: Eats pests like aphids, making them great for gardens
