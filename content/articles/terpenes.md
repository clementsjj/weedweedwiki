**Terpenes** are one of the most widely found classes of organic molecules in all of nature. They give plants their distinct smells and tastes, and play a crucial role in the [cannabis](cannabis) experience beyond just [THC](thc) and [CBD](cbd).

## Overview

Terpenes are organic compounds naturally produced by plants which create unique smells and flavors. Their natural functions include:

- **Attracting pollinators** - Drawing bees, butterflies, and other beneficial insects
- **Deterring pests** - Repelling herbivores and harmful insects
- **Protection** - Guarding against fungus, microbes, and UV rays
- **Communication** - Signaling between plants

In the cannabis world, terpenes create unique profiles that change how flower tastes and feels during [consumption](consumption).

## The Entourage Effect

Terpenes don't just provide flavor—they work synergistically with [cannabinoids](cannabinoids) like [THC](thc) and [CBD](cbd) in what researchers call the "entourage effect." This interaction may influence:

- The intensity and character of the high
- Therapeutic benefits
- Duration of effects
- Overall experience

## Preserving Terpenes

Terpenes are volatile compounds that can degrade with:

- **Heat** - High temperatures cause evaporation
- **Light** - UV exposure breaks down molecules
- **Air** - Oxidation degrades terpene profiles
- **Time** - Gradual loss over storage

Proper storage in cool, dark, airtight containers helps preserve the terpene profile.

## Did You Know

Drug detection dogs are trained to smell terpenes, not [THC](thc) itself. This is why they can detect [cannabis](cannabis) even in sealed containers—the aromatic terpenes are difficult to fully contain.
