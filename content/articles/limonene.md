**Limonene** is one of the most common [terpenes](terpenes) found in nature and the second most abundant terpene in [cannabis](cannabis). It's responsible for the bright, citrusy aroma found in lemons, oranges, and many uplifting cannabis strains.

## Aroma Profile

Limonene produces a distinctive citrus scent:

- Lemon
- Orange
- Grapefruit
- Tangerine
- Fresh and zesty

The aroma is often described as bright, clean, and energizing.

## Natural Sources

Limonene is abundant in citrus fruits and many other plants:

- **Citrus peels** - Lemons, oranges, limes, grapefruits
- **Cannabis** - Many sativa-dominant strains
- **Juniper** - Contributes to gin's flavor
- **Rosemary** - Part of its aromatic profile
- **Peppermint** - Adds to its fresh scent
- **Dill** - Herbal applications

## Potential Benefits

Research suggests limonene may have several therapeutic properties:

- **Mood elevation** - May reduce stress and anxiety
- **Anti-inflammatory** - Shown to reduce inflammation markers
- **Antioxidant** - Protects cells from oxidative stress
- **Digestive aid** - Used traditionally for heartburn and gastric reflux
- **Antibacterial** - Activity against certain bacteria
- **Antifungal** - May inhibit fungal growth

## Limonene in Cannabis

Strains high in limonene typically produce uplifting, energetic effects. Some strains known for elevated limonene content include:

- Super Lemon Haze
- Durban Poison
- Jack Herer
- Lemon Skunk
- Tangie
- Wedding Cake

### Effects Profile

Cannabis strains high in limonene are often associated with:

- Elevated mood
- Stress relief
- Mental clarity
- Energetic feelings
- Creative inspiration

## The Entourage Effect

Limonene works synergistically with other [terpenes](terpenes) and [cannabinoids](cannabinoids). When combined with [THC](thc) or [CBD](cbd), it may enhance mood-elevating and anti-anxiety effects. This synergy is part of what researchers call the "entourage effect."

## Industrial Uses

Beyond cannabis, limonene has many commercial applications:

- **Cleaning products** - Natural solvent and degreaser
- **Food flavoring** - Citrus flavoring agent
- **Fragrances** - Perfumes and cosmetics
- **Aromatherapy** - Essential oil applications
- **Pesticides** - Natural insect repellent

## Isomers

Limonene exists in two mirror-image forms:

| Isomer | Also Called | Aroma |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| D-limonene | (+)-limonene | Orange, citrus |
| L-limonene | (-)-limonene | Piney, turpentine |

The D-limonene form is what gives citrus fruits and most cannabis strains their characteristic orange/lemon scent.
