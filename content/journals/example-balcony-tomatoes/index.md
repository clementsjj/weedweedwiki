## The Plan

Growing cherry tomatoes on my small apartment balcony. I've got about 4 hours of direct sunlight, so we'll see how it goes. Using 5-gallon fabric pots with organic soil.

**Variety:** Sweet 100 Cherry Tomatoes
**Medium:** Fox Farm Ocean Forest
**Containers:** 2x 5-gallon fabric pots

## Week 1 - March 15

Started seeds indoors under a cheap grow light. Planted 6 seeds, hoping for at least 2 good seedlings.

<!-- Images go right alongside this file! Example: -->
<!-- ![Seedlings sprouting](seedlings.jpg) -->

## Week 3 - March 29

4 out of 6 seeds sprouted! The two strongest seedlings are about 3 inches tall now. Going to transplant to larger containers this weekend.

## Week 6 - April 19

Moved them outside to the balcony. They seemed a bit shocked at first but perked up after a few days. Starting to see some real growth now.

## Week 10 - May 17

First flowers! Little yellow blossoms appearing. I've been feeding with a tomato fertilizer every other week.

## Week 14 - June 14

Tons of green tomatoes on the vine. The plants are about 3 feet tall now. Had to add some stakes for support.

## Week 16 - June 28

First harvest! Got about 20 ripe cherry tomatoes. They taste amazing - way better than store bought.

<!-- ![First harvest](harvest.jpg) -->

## Lessons Learned

- 4 hours of sun is enough for tomatoes, but more would be better
- Fabric pots dry out fast - water daily in summer
- Should have started staking earlier
- Definitely doing this again next year
