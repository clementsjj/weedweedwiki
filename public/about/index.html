About WeedWeed.Wiki

WeedWeed.Wiki is a community-driven encyclopedia dedicated to cannabis information. Our goal is to provide accurate, accessible, and comprehensive information about cannabis in a wiki-style format.

Every page on the site can be found here: all articles

What We Cover

  • Cannabis species and strains
  • Cannabinoids like THC and CBD
  • Terpenes and flavor profiles
  • Growing and cultivation
  • Consumption methods
  • And much more…

Wiki Philosophy

To be a good wiki, pages should be:

  • Editable by the community
  • Editable in the browser
  • Version history is viewable and accessible
  • Navigate fluidly between links

The flat file structure with all pages in the same “articles” directory helps with fluid navigation. With wikis, links provide structure rather than directories. Frontmatter provides organizational metadata without separating related topics into different directories.

Technology

This site is built with Hugo , a fast static site generator. Content is written in Markdown and hosted on GitHub, enabling version control and collaborative editing.
